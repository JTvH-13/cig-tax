## A2

- The tax increased in April 2017. A 28% increase in revenue is less than what would be predicted if 9 out of the 12 years of 2027 were included in the revenue measure and the actual reported yearly revenue is about 60.1% of the expected (multiplying 2017 price by 2017 packs sold). This could be due to the U.S. gov't year not corresponding to a calendar year such that only a few months at the end of the 2017 'year' used here included the higher tax rate.

## A3

What "year" means for each measure:

- packs_pc (pack sales per capita): fiscal year 2017 (July 1, 2016 - June 30, 2017)
- revenue (gross tax revenue): same fiscal year
- price (average cost per pack): snapshot of weighted average retail price on November 1, 2017
- state_tax: rate in effect on November 1st, not a full-year average

This result confirms that for revenue, 9 months of '2017' were prior to the tax increase and only 3 (April - June 2017) at $2.87. That is why revenue only rose 28 percent in '2017'. Additionally, because price is marked as the higher price (whole $2 added) for '2017,' there is an overestimation of average price across the calendar year of 2017. The same is true for 2017 state_tax, which is stated to already be $2.87 but this value only marks one day of the year (Nov. 1) rather than an average across the whole year.

Such that:

- the price comparison is 2016 vs. 2017
- the pack sales comparison is 2017 vs. 2018.
